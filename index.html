Features

Your best playing,
automatically saved

Improv Helper stays out of your way and catches the moments that matter.

🎹

Auto-Capture

When you stop playing, Improv Helper scores the phrase and saves it automatically if it's worth keeping. No buttons, no interruptions to your flow.

📊

Smart Phrase Scoring

Each phrase is rated 0–100 based on note count, pitch variety, duration, and rhythmic feel. Set your own threshold so only the good stuff gets saved.

⏺

Manual Recording

Tap Record any time to capture exactly what you're playing, regardless of score. Perfect for fragments, experiments, or ideas you just want to hold onto.

📚

Built-in Motif Library

Every captured phrase lives in a persistent library. Browse, play back, and delete entries without ever leaving the app.

💾

DAW-Ready MIDI Files

Phrases are saved as standard .mid files. Drop them straight into Ableton, Logic, FL Studio, Reaper — anything that reads MIDI.

🔌

Any MIDI Device

Keyboards, synthesizers, controllers, drum pads — if it sends MIDI, Improv Helper hears it. No special drivers beyond what your interface already uses.

How It Works

Three steps from download
to captured ideas

No complicated setup. Just play.

1

Connect Your Keyboard

Plug in your MIDI keyboard or controller and launch the app. Your devices appear automatically — pick one from the dropdown and hit Connect.

2

Play Freely

Improvise however you like. Improv Helper watches in the background, scoring everything you play in real time — you'll never know it's there.

3

Your Library Grows

When a phrase hits your score threshold, it's captured automatically. Or hit Record any time to save on demand. Every phrase becomes a .mid file waiting in your library.
